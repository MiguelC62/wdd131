# Arte Claudia

Online gallery of original paintings by Claudia. WDD 131 final project, stage 1.

## Structure

```
project/
├── index.html           Home page
├── gallery.html         Gallery with category filter and sorting
├── about.html           Artist story, techniques and buying questions
├── contact.html         Contact and commission request form
├── thankyou.html        Confirmation page after the contact form
├── siteplan.html        Site plan document
├── styles/
│   ├── styles.css       Shared styles for the site pages
│   └── siteplan.css     Styles for the site plan
├── scripts/
│   ├── main.js          Menu and footer (every page)
│   ├── home.js          Featured artwork and new work alerts
│   ├── gallery.js       Gallery filter and sorting
│   ├── contact.js       Contact form (painting list and fields by reason)
│   ├── thankyou.js      Message counter on the confirmation page
│   ├── cards.js         Artwork card helpers (home, gallery, contact)
│   └── artworks.js      Artwork data
└── images/              Logo, wireframes, hero and artwork images (WebP)
```

Artwork titles and images are Claudia's real paintings. Three works are marked as sold and show no price.
