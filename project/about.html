About Claudia

Claudia paints the mountains, light and culture of the Andes. Each painting begins with a place she knows well and ends as a unique original, signed by her hand.

Her Story

Claudia was born in Argentina and grew up surrounded by the landscapes of the Andes: snowy peaks, wide valleys, lakes that change color through the day and markets full of woven textiles. These places are the starting point of almost every painting she makes.

She graduated as a painter from the School of Art in Salta, Argentina, and went on to take courses at the Museum of Contemporary Art. She has specialized in oil painting and landscapes.

Her Work

  • Inspiration

    Andean landscapes, colonial streets and still lifes of everyday objects, along with abstract works that turn familiar subjects into color and rhythm.

  • Technique

    Oil painting is her specialty, along with acrylic and mixed media on canvas. Every work is painted by hand; there are no prints or copies.

  • Authenticity

    Every painting is signed by Claudia and delivered with a certificate of authenticity that includes the title, size, technique and year.

Buying Questions

Does each painting include a certificate of authenticity?

Yes. Every original is signed by Claudia and comes with a signed certificate of authenticity.

How are prices set?

Prices depend on the size of the painting. The size, technique and price of each work are listed in the gallery.

Can I commission a custom painting?

Yes. Claudia paints custom works for homes, offices, hotels and restaurants. Send a commission request on the contact page with the size and the theme you have in mind.

Do you ship outside the country?

Shipping options, delivery times and costs depend on the painting and the destination. They are confirmed when you ask about a painting.

How do I pay?

Payment methods are confirmed when you ask about a painting. Online purchases will be added in a later stage of the site.

Have a Question?

Ask about a painting or request a commission, and Claudia will answer you personally.
